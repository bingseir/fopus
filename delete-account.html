# Delete your Fopus account You can permanently delete your Fopus account and all data associated with it. ## Option 1 — In the app 1. Open **Fopus** 2. Go to **Settings** 3. Tap **Delete account** 4. Confirm Your account and data are removed immediately. ## Option 2 — By email If you have already uninstalled the app, email **bingseir@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Delete my Fopus account"**, sent from the email address registered to your account. Requests are processed within 30 days. ## What gets deleted - Your account and login credentials - All study subjects you created - All recorded study sessions and their history - All settings and preferences All of the above are deleted permanently and cannot be recovered. ## What is retained Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you or your account may be retained for analytics purposes. If you have an active subscription, purchase records are retained by Google Play as required by law and tax regulations. Deleting your Fopus account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in the Google Play Store. ## Contact Questions about deletion or your data: **bingseir@gmail.com**
